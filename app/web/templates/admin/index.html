What the three permissions mean

  • read — the live article and the version history of that path.
  • write — create, update, move, archive and unarchive. Includes read.
  • own — everything, on that place and beneath it, including granting any of these to anyone. Owners administer their part of the tree here.

Editing is not redaction: a prior version still says what it said to anyone who can read the path. To put something out of reach, move it; to erase it entirely, open {{ prefix }}/admin/delete/<path> for the break-glass runbook — it lists the versions and the commands, and deletes nothing itself.
