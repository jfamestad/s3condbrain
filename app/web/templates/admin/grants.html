Grant access

  • read — the live article and the version history of that path.
  • write — create, update, move, archive and unarchive there; includes read.
  • own — everything on that place and beneath it, and they can grant any of these to anyone beneath it.

A grant on a folder covers everything beneath it; a grant on one article covers that article only. There are no deny rules — to carve out an exception, move the article. Editing is not redaction: readers of a path can read its history.
