Every grant names a place in the tree, so moving an article changes who can read it. A move that would give anyone access is refused to agents; it is done here, where you see who gains and who loses before you confirm. The old path keeps a pointer to the new one, and the article's earlier versions stay behind it.

Absolute article paths, lowercase, ending in .md. You need write access on both, and you must own the destination's folder.
